 # Generate vignettes and analyze data of vignette study for part III

As a first try we employed the [deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1](https://huggingface.co/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1) reasoning large language model to systematically generate text about future material systems using a combination of pairs of identified partitions. These texts were evaluated by (a) experts to evaluate the quality of the texts and (b) in a second step by laypersons. However in (a) the text were deemed as to unsystematic and we generated the text purley by experts.
